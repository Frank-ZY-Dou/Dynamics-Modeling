Context, then prediction
The first four frames at the chosen step size are given (orange on the timeline). Every later frame is predicted by the model from its own previous predictions.
Step size
One model predicts at all three step sizes. With step size s it advances s simulation frames (240 per second) per prediction: 476 predictions for the 2-second scene at s = 1, 44 at s = 10. Playback is in real time at 1×; between predictions the poses are interpolated for display.
What is drawn
The predictions only, on six test scenes per dataset chosen for low prediction error among the busier half of the test set. Each object is drawn with its render mesh, moved by the rigid motion the model predicts for its collision mesh.
